Common inquiries

Youth & family FAQ

Do parents pay admission if they only supervise?

No. Non-climbing adult chaperones and party parents enter free. You only pay for a pass and gear if you choose to climb.

Can I drop off my 12-year-old?

Unsupervised drop-offs are only allowed for enrolled classes, camps, and team training. Casual open climbing requires active on-site adult supervision.

How does team placement work?

Coaches use movement evaluations to assess coachability, safety awareness, teamwork, and work ethic. Placement depends on available roster space.

Can my child bring personal climbing gear?

Yes. Personal equipment is welcome and our front desk team can perform a quick visual inspection before use.
