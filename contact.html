Quick answers

Before you arrive.

Do I need a reservation?

No reservation is required for open climbing. Groups of seven or more should contact us ahead of time.

Where should first-time visitors start?

Begin with the First Visit Guide, sign your waiver online, then check in at the front desk.

Who should I contact for a party?

Use our parties and groups page so we can collect the right date and group details.

Can I ask about membership here?

Yes. Visit Member Services for account requests or call the front desk.
