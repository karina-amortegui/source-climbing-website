Frequently asked questions.

Are rentals included?

No. Rentals are separate from memberships and passes. See current rental pricing on the Rates & Rentals page.

Where can I purchase a membership?

Use the plan-specific purchase links where available, or contact the front desk for plans marked front-desk purchase.

Can I manage my account on this page?

No. This is an information and sales page. Current members should use Member Services until the verified Redpoint HQ destination is approved.

Which details still need owner approval?

Guest-pass policies, freezes, cancellation timing, early-access hours, and any additional member benefits should be confirmed before being published as policy.
