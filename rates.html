Questions

Before you buy.

Are rentals included?

Rentals are not included with day passes, 10-day passes, or memberships. Add only the gear you need.

Are group discounts available?

We do not offer group discounts. For groups of seven or more, we recommend scheduling a belayer. Contact info@sourceclimbing.com for more information.
