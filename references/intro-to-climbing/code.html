Class Blueprint

What You Will Learn

check
Harness Anatomy & Double-Pass Fitting

Proper structural fit above the iliac crest, gear loop orientation, and thorough visual double-check routines before tying in.

check
Figure-8 Follow-Through & Backup

Step-by-step instruction on creating a clean, dressable figure-eight knot tied through both hard tie-in points, secured with a backup.

check
ATC Belay & The PBUS Sequence

Mastering the modern industry-standard PBUS sequence ensuring the brake hand never breaks contact with the control strand.

check
Commands & Mutual Partner Checks

Standard climbing communication protocols and the essential physical-visual 5-point buddy inspection before every climb.

check
Simulated Fall Catching & Smooth Lowering

Dynamic body positioning, absorbing rope tension when a climber falls, and maintaining even brake friction to lower partners comfortably to the floor.

hiking

Who It's For

  • • First-Time Climbers: Anyone wanting to learn how ropes and modern gym walls operate safely.
  • • Climbing Partners: Couples and friends who want the freedom to belay each other without gym staff.
  • • Parents & Guardians: Learn how to belay your youth climbers during family hours and team meets.
backpack

What To Bring

  • • Athletic Clothing: Comfortable gym attire that permits full hip movement under harness leg loops.
  • • Socks: Thin socks if you plan on using our sanitized demo rental climbing shoes.
  • • Water Bottle: Stay hydrated; we have filtered bottle-filling stations throughout the gym.
