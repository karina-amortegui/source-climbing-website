What to Bring & Arrival Steps

We want your first five minutes to feel effortless. Here is the checklist to prepare for your climb.

backpack

What to Bring

checkroom

Athletic, Flexible Clothing

Wear clothes you can move freely in—sweatpants, leggings, or athletic shorts.

footprint

Clean Socks for Rentals

Rental shoes require thin athletic socks. Bring your own pair; socks also available for $2 at the counter.

badge

Government Photo ID

Adult climbers need a valid government-issued ID to verify digital waiver submission.

water_bottle

Water Bottle

Cold filtered bottle refilling stations are located on both gym floors.

timeline

What to Expect on Arrival

1

Check In at the Front Desk

Our staff verifies your digital waiver, processes admission passes, and answers any immediate questions.

2

Gear Fitting & Cubby Storage

We fit you into proper climbing shoes and harnesses. Drop your jackets and shoes in our free day cubbies.

3

15-Minute Safety Walkthrough

A staff member walks you through bouldering fall technique, gym etiquette, and auto-belay clipping procedures.

4

Climb for the Rest of the Day

You're cleared! Explore color-coded routes and take relaxing breaks in our lounge whenever you need.

Safety & Community Care

Youth Supervision & Gym Rules

Our safety culture protects all climbers. We expect everyone to treat the walls, pads, and fellow athletes with respect.

escalator_warning

1:2 Ratio for Children Under 14

One adult (18+) can actively supervise up to two children under 14 at any time. Supervisors must stay within arm's reach and watch landings.

airline_seat_recline_extra

Fall Zone Awareness

Never walk or sit on the safety pads underneath active climbers. Always look up before crossing between bouldering lanes.

lock_reset

Auto-Belay Double-Check

Youth under 14 must have an adult partner verify and clip the carabiner gate to the harness belay loop before leaving the floor.

Got Questions?

First-Timer Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before stepping through our doors.

No reservations are required for open climbing! You can walk in anytime during regular gym hours. Reservations are only needed if you are scheduling a structured introductory technique class, summer camp, or private group event with staff belayers.
Many of our regular members started with a fear of heights! You are in 100% control of how high you climb. You can start on our bouldering walls just 2–3 feet off the padded mat, traverse horizontally across low walls, or climb only halfway up auto-belays until you build confidence.
Auto-belays are mechanical safety systems mounted at the top of the wall. You simply clip the locking carabiner to your harness belay loop. As you ascend, the machine automatically retracts the lanyard. When you let go or reach the top, magnetic braking smoothly lowers you down at a gentle walking pace.
Yes! Bouldering is climbing on shorter 14-foot walls over seamless 16-inch safety foam pads with no ropes or harness needed. All you need are climbing shoes and chalk. It is our most popular walk-in discipline for solo athletes and friends.
Absolutely. Every day pass includes full in-and-out privileges for that entire calendar day. Grab lunch at one of downtown Vancouver’s neighborhood cafes and return to keep climbing until close.
