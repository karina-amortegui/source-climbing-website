Frequently Asked Questions

How do my 2 free monthly guest passes work? expand_more
Active monthly EFT and Annual members receive two guest passes every calendar month. Rental gear is included for your guest's first visit.
Can I share my punch pass or transfer passes? expand_more
Punch passes may be shared with accompanying partners. Monthly guest passes are non-transferable and require member check-in.
What if I need an immediate medical freeze? expand_more
For unexpected injuries, we waive both the 25th deadline and $10 fee upon provision of a medical provider note.
Can I unfreeze early if my plans change? expand_more
Yes, reactivate any time at the front counter. Dues will simply be prorated for the remainder of that billing cycle.
Have other questions? Check at our desk or reach out below.
