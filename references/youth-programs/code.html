SAFETY & PREPARATION

Guardian Guidelines & Arrival Prep

Everything you need to know before stepping onto the mats. Adhering to these standard procedures ensures a seamless, safe experience for youth climbers and supervisors alike.

supervisor_account

1:2 Supervision Rule

Any visitor under age 14 must have an active adult (18+) supervising them on the padded surfaces at a 1:2 ratio. Chaperones actively spot, guide route etiquette, and stay within arm's reach.

Important: Non-climbing chaperones do not pay general gym admission.
assignment_turned_in

Parent Smartwaiver

Washington State regulations require that each minor climber have a digital liability waiver submitted directly by their legal parent or court-appointed guardian. Friends, carpool drivers, or extended family cannot execute waivers for minors.

Complete online beforehand to avoid delays at front desk check-in.

COMMON INQUIRIES

Youth & Family FAQ

Quick answers to the questions Vancouver parents ask our front desk team most often.

help_outline

Do parents need to pay admission if they are only supervising?

No! Non-climbing adult chaperones and party parents enter completely free of charge. You only pay for a day pass and gear rental if you choose to climb on the walls yourself.

help_outline

Can I drop my 12-year-old off while I run downtown errands?

Unsupervised drop-offs are only allowed for enrolled youth classes, camps, and team training blocks. Casual open climbing requires active on-site adult supervision at a 1:2 ratio for anyone under 14.

help_outline

How does youth climbing team placement work?

Our coaching staff hosts friendly movement evaluations to assess coachability, safety awareness, teamwork, and work ethic. Tryouts take place bi-annually and placements depend on open roster vacancies.

help_outline

Can my child bring their own climbing shoes and harness?

Yes, absolutely. Personal equipment is thoroughly welcomed. Our front desk staff will conduct a quick visual inspection of harness webbing and buckle condition during your initial check-in.
