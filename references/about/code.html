The Heritage

From Passion Project to Community Anchor

Before Source opened its doors on Main Street in 2011, Clark County climbers had a single option: brave rush-hour traffic over the Interstate Bridge into Portland just to catch an evening session. We believed Southwest Washington deserved its own home for climbing—a place authentic enough for seasoned rock climbers, yet patient and encouraging enough for a kid tying their first figure-eight knot.

We set out to build a facility centered around craftsmanship, human connection, and genuine safety education. Today, Source is more than climbing walls: it is a bustling downtown hub where neighbors run into each other, local high school teams train alongside masters athletes, and outdoor friendships are forged that last a lifetime.

Safety Through Education

We adhere to gold-standard PBUS belay protocols, conduct rigorous daily equipment inspections, and teach transferable real-world rope craft that keeps you protected outdoors.

Craft in Routesetting

We treat routesetting as dynamic choreography. Every route is thoughtfully stripped, washed, and set on rotating sectors each week to ensure biomechanically safe, diverse movement.
