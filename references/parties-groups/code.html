Smooth Day-Of Experience

What to Expect & Prep Checklist

We want you to arrive completely relaxed. Review these four pillars so you and your climbers are primed for wall time.

01

Zero Belaying Hassle

Dedicated Source staff manage all rope tie-ins, safety checks, and belaying. Parents and chaperones are free to relax, cheer, and take photos.

photo_cameraSpectator passes are 100% free
02

Smartwaiver Required

Every participant (youth and adults) must have a digital waiver submitted by a parent or legal guardian prior to arriving at the facility.

Pre-send waiverSign Digital →
03

Private Mezzanine

You receive designated table space overlooking the gym for the final 30 minutes. Feel free to bring pizza, cupcakes, juice boxes, and snacks.

kitchenMini-fridge storage upon request
04

What Climbers Wear

Athletic attire and clean socks (mandatory for rentals). Tie back long hair, remove rings or jewelry, and bring a refillable water bottle.

checkLocker cubbies provided
