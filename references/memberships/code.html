Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our billing, freezing, cancellation policies, and guest access.

Can I freeze my monthly membership if I get injured or travel?ac_unit

Yes! Monthly FLEX members can freeze their membership for any reason for just $10/month instead of paying the full dues or re-paying the initiation fee later. Freezes must be requested by the 25th of the month via our Member Portal. Annual members receive one 2-month freeze included per year.

How do the 2 free monthly guest passes work?group

Recurring FLEX members receive 2 guest passes loaded into their profile on the 1st of each calendar month. Bring a friend who is new to climbing or hasn't visited Source in the last 6 months! Guest passes include gym day access (rentals are separate) and do not roll over month-to-month.

What is your cancellation policy? Are there contract buyouts?cancel

We do not believe in locking climbers into multi-year contracts. To cancel your FLEX recurring membership, simply submit an online cancellation form by the 25th of the current month to avoid being billed for the following cycle. There are zero cancellation or exit fees.

When does monthly billing occur?calendar_month

Monthly dues are automatically processed on the 1st of every month via Electronic Funds Transfer (credit card or debit card on file). If you sign up mid-month, your first payment is prorated for the remaining days of that month.

Can I share my 10-Visit Punch Pass with my family or friends?share

Yes! Unlike individual memberships, 10-Visit Punch Passes are 100% shareable. You can bring a friend or family member and use multiple punches in the same visit. Furthermore, punches never expire.
