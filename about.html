Our ethos & origin

A gym built by climbers,
for the community.

Founded with a conviction that indoor climbing should be inclusive, challenging, and deeply welcoming. Source is Southwest Washington’s gathering place for first-timers, seasoned crushers, and everyone in between.

“All Are Climbers Here.”Our guiding belief, every day.
Community hall & gathering area · Main Street
Our story

From passion project to community anchor.

Before Source opened its doors on Main Street, Clark County climbers had to cross the Interstate Bridge for an evening session. We believed Southwest Washington deserved its own home for climbing—a place authentic enough for seasoned climbers and encouraging enough for a kid tying their first knot.

Today, Source is more than climbing walls. It is a downtown hub where neighbors meet, high school teams train alongside masters athletes, and outdoor friendships take root.
