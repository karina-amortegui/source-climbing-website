Got questions?

First-Timer FAQ

Everything you need to know before stepping through our doors.

Do I need a reservation before visiting?

No reservations are required for open climbing. Walk in anytime during regular gym hours; reservations are only needed for classes, camps, and private group events.

What if I’m afraid of heights?

You control how high you climb. Start on low bouldering walls or climb only partway up an auto-belay while you build confidence.

Can I just boulder without ropes?

Yes. Bouldering uses shorter walls over thick safety padding, so you only need climbing shoes and chalk—no rope or harness required.

Can I leave and return on the same day pass?

Absolutely. Day passes include in-and-out privileges for the full calendar day.
