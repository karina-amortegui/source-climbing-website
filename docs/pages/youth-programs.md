# Youth Programs

Route: `/youth`. Navigation: Youth & Families. Status: proposed page-family specification.

## Purpose and audience

Help parents distinguish casual visits, instruction, camps, teams, and parties. Primary action depends on the selected program: **View Program**, **View Dates & Register**, or **Request a Party**.

## Section order

1. Family introduction and casual-visit guidance.
2. Program pathways: youth classes, camps, recreational/competitive teams, parties.
3. Comparable age and prerequisite summaries.
4. Guardian waiver, supervision, and arrival preparation.
5. FAQs and contact support.

## Supporting pages

Preserve or map `/youth-classes`, `/climbing-team`, and `/birthday-parties`. Camps may have a dedicated detail page after migration review. Team assessments and class/camp registration retain verified provider destinations.

Program records include ages, prerequisites, duration, schedule, price, equipment, supervision arrangements, and booking action. Show coach approval or assessment requirements without implying guaranteed placement. Do not reactivate discontinued programs through migrated links.

## Party and group inquiry

Show package, capacity, available time windows, equipment/staffing, preparation, and confirmed cancellation terms. Resolve the previously noted Sunday timing discrepancy before publication.

Form fields: contact name, email, phone, preferred date, participant count, age range, preferred contact method, notes. Proposed required fields: name, email, preferred date, participant count, age range, and contact preference; phone is required when phone contact is selected. Staff must confirm the final required fields and accepted participant limits.

Use Request a Party, not Book Now. Validate dates/counts without inventing available slots. On successful delivery, confirm receipt and explain staff follow-up with an approved response expectation. Preserve values on recoverable failure and offer a contact alternative. Do not collect unnecessary child identity or medical details in a general inquiry.

## Responsive behavior and acceptance

- Parents can choose by age and experience without reading every program page.
- Costs, prerequisites, and guardian requirements are visible before registration.
- Cards and inquiry fields stack in logical mobile order.
- Team assessments do not imply placement; party requests do not imply reservations.
- Inquiry delivery, errors, and correct provider links are verified.

## Confirmation needed

Active programs, age categories, supervision, seasonal dates, team assessment rules, party capacity/times, inquiry recipients, and response expectations. Follow [website-spec.md](../website-spec.md) and [theme.md](../theme.md).
