# About

Proposed route: `/about`. Navigation: About. Status: proposed page-family specification.

## Purpose and audience

Introduce the gym's people, values, and facility while helping visitors take a practical next step. Primary action: **Plan Your Visit** → `/first-visit`. Secondary actions: meet the team, explore the facility, view careers, contact the gym.

## Section order

1. Source's story and welcoming community message.
2. Mission and values, based on approved existing material.
3. Facility overview and climbing disciplines.
4. Team introduction and profile links.
5. Selected history/milestones.
6. Careers/application information if current.
7. Visit and contact actions.

## Supporting content and behavior

Retain or deliberately map `/our-story`, `/our-team`, and `/facility`. Detailed facility rules remain available from visitor journeys, not solely inside brand storytelling. Consolidate repeated values/history without losing useful material.

Confirm staff names, roles, photographs, and permissions. Facility statistics and milestones need verification. Careers should show current openings only; preserve the downloadable application/email process unless a replacement workflow is specifically approved. Verify downloads and embedded videos and provide accessible alternatives where needed.

## Responsive behavior and states

Use readable prose widths, stacked team cards, and meaningful image alternatives. Avoid hover-only biographies. If no roles are open, say so using approved copy rather than presenting an expired vacancy. Failed video embeds retain a direct link when available.

## Acceptance

- Story, values, and team details are approved and not duplicated inconsistently.
- Facility information and rules are easy to reach.
- Careers and downloadable materials are current and usable.
- Visitors have an obvious route to visit planning and contact.

## Confirmation needed

Approved story, current staff/roles, image rights, facility details, openings, application document, and final supporting routes. Follow [website-spec.md](../website-spec.md) and [theme.md](../theme.md).
