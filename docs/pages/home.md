# Home

Route: `/`. Navigation: logo/home. Status: proposed page specification.

## Purpose and audience

Orient new visitors while giving parents, prospective members, and returning climbers a quick route to their task. Primary action: **Plan Your First Visit** → `/first-visit`. Secondary action: **Explore Classes** → `/classes`.

## Section order

1. Optional current announcement and shared header.
2. Photographic hero: “All Are Climbers Here,” a short Vancouver gym introduction, and the two primary pathways.
3. Practical information strip: shared hours, location, and Rates & Rentals link.
4. Audience pathways: New to Climbing, Youth & Families, Become a Member.
5. First-visit overview: choose an experience, complete a waiver, arrive for orientation.
6. Featured classes with ages, experience level, duration, and booking/detail actions.
7. Membership introduction linking to plan comparison.
8. Upcoming community events with real dates/status.
9. Facility/community imagery and shared footer.

## Content and functionality

Reuse approved business information, selected programs, and upcoming event records. Gym/content owners select photography and featured offerings. Do not display invented reviews, attendance statistics, or popularity badges.

Class and event cards link to detail pages before registration where preparation information is necessary. Global waiver, member-service, and gift links remain accessible. Track pathway and provider-handoff clicks separately from purchases.

## Responsive behavior and states

Stack pathways and cards on mobile without carousels required to discover content. Keep hero copy legible independently of the photograph. Omit an unused announcement. If no upcoming events exist, show a short empty message and Community link; failed feeds must not show fabricated event cards.

## Acceptance

- First-visit, classes, youth, and membership paths are easy to locate on desktop and mobile.
- Repeated hours/prices match shared records.
- Hero text and controls retain contrast and keyboard focus.
- Featured programs and events have valid destinations and current status.

## Confirmation needed

Hero photography rights, approved introductory copy, featured programs, and current announcements. Follow [website-spec.md](../website-spec.md) and [theme.md](../theme.md).
