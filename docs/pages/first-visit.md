# First Visit

Route: `/first-visit`. Navigation: Visit. Status: proposed page specification.

## Purpose and audience

Answer what a visit involves, what it costs, and how to prepare. Serve individual adults, families, and groups. Main actions: **Sign Waiver** → verified Smartwaiver destination; **Find a Beginner Class** → `/classes`.

## Section order

1. Welcoming introduction and adult/family/group pathways.
2. Walk-in climbing versus introductory class comparison.
3. Admission and rental cost summary using shared pricing.
4. What to bring and what happens at check-in/orientation.
5. Waiver and guardian requirements with signing action.
6. Youth supervision and group reservation guidance.
7. Hours, directions, and practical arrival information.
8. Concise FAQs and final next actions.

## Content and functionality

Clearly distinguish a walk-in visit from a reserved class. Explain rental inclusion/exclusion and any additional costs. Link to full facility rules without burying preparation essentials. Group visitors should reach an inquiry/contact destination with accurate reservation expectations.

Preserve external signing and its real verification flow; clicking the waiver link does not prove completion. Do not rebuild signatures or legal agreements in a local form. Operations staff must confirm waiver, ID, supervision, age, orientation, and group policies.

## Responsive behavior and states

Use stacked comparisons and readable checklists on mobile. If audience selectors are used, they must be keyboard accessible and must not hide universal requirements. Provide direct provider/contact fallback when signing is unavailable. FAQs use semantic disclosure controls.

## Acceptance

- A beginner can identify admission/rental needs and whether a reservation is necessary.
- Parents can find applicable preparation and supervision requirements.
- Signing and booking links reach the correct providers without implying completed registration.
- Policies and hours match their shared sources.

## Confirmation needed

Current costs, group threshold, party-area restrictions, supervision language, arrival/ID instructions, and waiver return URLs. Follow [website-spec.md](../website-spec.md) and [theme.md](../theme.md).
