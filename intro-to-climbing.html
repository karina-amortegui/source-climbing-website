Class blueprint

What you’ll learn

✓

Harness anatomy & fitting

Proper fit, gear loop orientation, and visual double-check routines before tying in.

✓

Figure-eight follow-through

Create a clean, dressable knot through both tie-in points with a secure backup.

✓

ATC belay & PBUS

Master the industry-standard sequence while keeping the brake hand in control.

✓

Commands & partner checks

Use standard communication and the essential five-point buddy check.

✓

Fall catching & smooth lowering

Practice body positioning, absorbing rope tension, and lowering a partner comfortably.

Who it’s for

First-time climbers, partners, and parents

Anyone who wants the freedom to belay safely with a partner during open gym hours.

What to bring

Athletic clothes, socks, and water

Wear comfortable clothing that allows full hip movement. Rental shoes and harnesses are included.
