Class blueprint

What you’ll learn

✓

Harness anatomy & fitting

Proper fit, gear-loop orientation, and visual double-check routines before tying in.

✓

Figure-eight follow-through

Create a clean, dressable knot through both tie-in points with a secure backup.

✓

ATC belay & PBUS

Practice the stated belay sequence while keeping the brake hand in control.

✓

Commands & partner checks

Use standard communication and the essential buddy-check routine.

✓

Fall catching & smooth lowering

Practice body positioning, rope tension, and lowering a partner comfortably.

Who it’s for

First-time climbers, partners, and parents

Anyone who wants to learn the foundations of belaying with a partner. Age and eligibility details require owner confirmation.

What to bring

Athletic clothes, socks, and water

Wear comfortable clothing that allows full hip movement. Whether rental shoes and harnesses are included requires confirmation.
